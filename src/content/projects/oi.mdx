## Overview
Oi is a multipurpose Discord bot, created by Shobhit Jain. I joined the development team in September of 2021.
The original repository had configuration tokens, and other private information commited into the repository,
and to play it safe, it was decided to create a new repository. Because of Shobhits time constraints, I have
been maintaining Oi.

For additional information about Oi, check out Shobhit's [post about Oi](https://www.shobhitjain.in/work/oi_bot)
on his website.
- You can also find him on [Discord](https://discord.com/users/531179463673774080), and [GitHub](https://github.com/Shobhits7)

As of October 2026, Oi currently serves approximately 640,000 users across 5,650 Discord servers.

## Features
Oi has over 90 commands, with its music commands being the most popular. Here are the key features.

### Music
The music module is powered by [Lavalink](https://github.com/lavalink-devs/Lavalink) with the [Wavelink](https://github.com/PythonistaGuild/Wavelink) library. It has many features, here are a few:
- Queue: The queue system allows multiple tracks to listed for playback. This system also supports shuffling, looping of the whole queue, and looping of one track.
  - Autoplay queue: When enabled, the autoplay system will automatically continue playing similar tracks, if the queue is empty.
- User Playlists: With playlists, you can group tracks from different sources, and give the group a custom name and image. You can later enqueue the whole playlist for quick and easy playback.
- DJ system: Enabled my default, the DJ system will only allow DJs, server mods, and people with a set DJ role to control the player.
- Interactive Player: Oi has an interactive player which uses Discord components to allow users to control the player with a simple click of a button. You can:
  rewind, play or pause, skip, toggle autoplay, enqueue a track, shuffle the queue, loop one/all, see lyrics of the current track.
  - The interactive player has three label settings which hide or show the interactive player buttons: No icons, Icons only (default), and Icons and labels.
- Filters: With filters, you can apply audio effects to the output of the player.

### Other Commands
Some other command categories that Oi offers are:
- Fun: games, and other fun commands like fun facts that bring great fun.
- Moderation: simple moderation commands.
- Utility: some utility commands and bot information commands.
